Cards Alex suspended because they were **painful to learn** (several are large near-synonym
groups). To be **unsuspended gradually**, a few at a time, rather than all at once — dumping
them back in together is what made them unmanageable before.

`deck:Vocab::* is:suspended`

Audio: all of these have recordings — the stale `needs-audio` tags were cleared 2026-08-22
(HyperTTS writes **one** file per card covering every merged sense, so a single `[sound:]` on a
5-sense card is normal, not evidence of a stale recording).

Caution when checking progress: **`is:learn` matches a card's TYPE, not its queue**, so these
suspended cards still match `is:learn` even though they can never appear. Any query about what
is genuinely being learned needs `-is:suspended` — see `scripts/export_learning_vocab.py`.
